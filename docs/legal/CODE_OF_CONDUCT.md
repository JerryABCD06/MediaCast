# Media Cast — Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

The Media Cast project welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and technical perspectives.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully;
- focus criticism on ideas and implementations rather than people;
- provide useful technical context when reporting problems;
- respect project maintainers and contributors;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, and personal attacks.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment or intimidation;
- threats of violence;
- discriminatory or hateful conduct;
- deliberate personal attacks;
- doxxing or publication of private information;
- sexual harassment;
- spam or malicious disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other reasonable action when community rules are violated.

For serious or urgent matters, contact the project maintainers privately.

This document is intentionally concise and may later be replaced with a more established open-source code of conduct.
